# Family Dent scoring rubric

Score the following dimensions. Use whole numbers when possible and explain unusual partial scores briefly.

| Category | Max | Evidence to check |
|---|---:|---|
| Greeting and professional opening | 10 | Greeting, Family Dent identification, administrator identification when appropriate, welcoming clarity, no unexplained waiting/confusion |
| Patient needs discovery | 20 | Reason, concern, service, urgency, relevant history, timing, and information needed for the next step; ask only relevant questions |
| Active listening and communication | 15 | Answers the actual question, understands the patient, avoids needless interruption, robotic/dismissive responses, and unsupported tone assumptions |
| Consultation / booking conversion | 20 | Consultation offer, active booking proposal, specific date/time, alternatives, value of consultation, appropriate close |
| Price handling | 10 | Accurate available price, no concealment, context/value, inclusions, uncertainty when diagnosis determines price, consultation offer |
| Objection handling | 10 | Acknowledge, clarify, respond with relevant information, propose a reasonable next step, appropriate follow-up |
| Closing and follow-up | 10 | Confirm details, date/time, preparation, contact instructions, next step, and follow-up when no booking |
| Patient experience / professionalism | 5 | Respect, clarity, helpfulness, professional language, no dismissive behavior |

Red flags are evidence-led indicators, not automatic deductions: missing greeting or clinic identification, abrupt opening, answering before understanding the need, excessive monosyllables, “I don’t know” without checking, “come whenever,” “check Instagram,” price-only handling where context is needed, no appointment offer or specific time, ignored objection, no next step, blaming, dismissive language, argument, unexplained silence, or unsupported emotion claims. Sighing and irritation require explicit audio/transcript evidence.

For each material issue write:

**Observation:** what happened, with an exact short quote only if present.

**Why it matters:** likely impact on patient experience or conversion, labeled as a potential impact rather than a certainty.

**Better approach:** a practical next behavior or phrase.
